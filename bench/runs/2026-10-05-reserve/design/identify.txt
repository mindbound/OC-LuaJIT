(a) THE MECHANISM TARGETED, AND THE EVIDENCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A refusal is the shim's message to the program that its data does not fit; lj52_gc_refused opens the
reserve tier (G/2 more past the cap) so that the catch, the message and the drop can be paid for, and
it does so whoever catches the error (map REPORT 1.0: "It does all three whoever ends up catching the
error.  That is the defect's mechanism").  The forensics say exactly who that is: every paint- and
recorder-absorbed refusal was a VERDICT refusal (F3: 2 303 + 25, win0 = 2) -- the verdict decided at
a proof is executed by the next crossing, whoever makes it -- and the opener of the second chance was
the probe's pcall(paint) in 2 169 of 2 192 cases and the JIT recorder in 23; the second chance ended
outside a handler only in the OpenOS-shaped timer probe, 21 of 228, every one the RESERVE-top verdict
at the timer's record or a checkArg closure, against 0 of 1 820 runs without a second chance (F4).
So the harm is not the absorbed refusal -- it is the G/2 (16 KiB at the floor, 256 KiB at 8 MB) that
it hands to a fill that never heard, and the reserve top's verdict landing wherever the next crossing
is.  The allocator can name two of the recipients exactly and cheaply: the recorder (map 1.1.4: the
innermost C frame is an lj_vm_cpcall frame, J->state not idle, vmstate not C/GC/EXIT; the instrument's
flag agreed with the --wrap=lj_vm_cpcall ground truth on all 31 000 refusals, F7) and a VM event
handler (HOOK_VMEVENT, map A4).  It cannot name the paint (map L8: sandbox code on the sandbox's
thread), so the paint-shaped recipient is bounded instead, by the smallest lever in the map's lifetime/
size family that leaves W8's recovery intact: the reserve is R past the refusal point (map L3), with
R = 4 KiB, once per episode.  L4 (close at the next resume) was rejected by analysis before
prototyping: after the paint's refusal the sandbox yields; closing the tier at the resume puts the
deciding proof at the dispatcher's own pack and the step's pcall closure -- the first crossings after
the resume, outside every handler -- so it would turn second chances into stalls at the burst top
(the shipped forensics' deterministic case F1 is exactly that shape: a verdict delivered to the
first growth after its proof, at event.timer's record).  L6 is rejected by evidence (d2-final 4.12).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(f) THE IN-MACHINE GATE, PRE-REGISTERED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The instrument first.  CAP-GC gains the four WALLSTATS values (absorbed=, strtab=, recording=,
od_credit=); the main session's instrumented native, if it lands first, already separates the
recorder, the kernel thread and the vmevent handler per refusal and should carry str.num/str.mask.
The gate: the amplified capacity probe of d2-final 8.2 (192 KB, scale 1.8, OCLJ_CAP_BATCH=10,
OCLJ_CAP_JUNK=24), THIS build (additive) in the JIT-on RECORD cell, 60 runs, interleaved with 20 runs
of the final-build V in the same cell (the same-chain control), plus the standard matrix at batch 100
(chain T's layout, V and this build, 48 runs each).  The pooled history for the cell is W + V: 21 of
30 runs with a paint-absorbed first refusal, 3 of them down.
Pass iff ALL hold:
  (1) teeth: >= 20 of the 60 runs show a second chance (OCLJCAPX pf >= 1 and CAP-GC refused >= 2),
      else the gate is VOID (the amplification did not transfer), not passed;
  (2) 0 DOWN + STALL + PROC-DEATH in the 60; against the pooled 3 of 30 this is one-sided Fisher
      p = 0.035, and against the same-chain V control it must not be worse;
  (3) every run's od_peak <= od_credit + window (the new bound, read from CAP-GC), and in every run
      with refused = 1, od_peak <= G/2 + window as before;
  (4) absorbed = 0 in every JIT-off run (the predicate cannot fire without a recorder) and
      recording = 0 in every run where no refusal was the program's -- the instrument's own sanity;
  (5) batch 100: 48 of 48 clean; capacity >= V's like for like in every cell within 1 % except the
      single-refusal cells, which must be equal (the burst tier is untouched); the known 1024 KB
      closure cell is judged against stock as before (0.98-0.99x stands);
  (6) last-five time within 1.10x of V's in every cell (fewer cycles are expected, not more).
A down at the reserve top in (2) with CAP-GC od_credit set fails the gate; a down with absorbed > 0
and the tier still BURST would mean rule 1 misfired and fails it too.  What the gate reports but
does not judge: strtab (expected 0; a nonzero count is the S1 finding and triggers the lj_str_resize
follow-up in (g)).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(g) COSTS AND RESIDUALS, HONESTLY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The second chance is not removed, it is bounded.  The paint (and in the machine OpenOS's
    dispatcher) still absorbs the first verdict refusal in 4-11 % of runs; those fills now go on for
    4 KiB + the window instead of G/2 + the window, and their end lands where the next crossing is:
    inside the batch in every one of the 1 030 hermetic second chances of this sweep (the 21 oe
    stalls gone), but the landing itself is THE WINDOW's mechanism, not this design's.  At larger G
    the saving is larger (256 KiB -> 8 KiB at 8 MB caps); the stall probability per second chance is
    what the machine gate measures.
  - Capacity: -0.06 % to -0.68 % per cell, all of it in second-chance runs (-12 KiB each at the G
    floor).  The one shape that loses more: a program whose single request is refused BELOW the burst
    top and retried unchanged (W9's class) -- the shipped shim's G/2 reserve let a 32 KiB holder
    doubling at cap + 2 KiB succeed on retry (ctl_rec.lua); rule 3 gives a sub-S refusal the burst top
    plus the window, so that retry is refused.  Stock refuses it too.  In the machine's JIT-on string
    cells at 192 KB this is the 16 KiB doubling at 2 048 objects: 5 of 15 W/V runs got it through
    the reserve and held 2 049; they will hold 2 048.
  - The recovery room after a SECOND refusal in the same episode (a program that caught one, kept
    filling, caught another) is the window only; after the first it is >= 4 KiB + the window.  A tty
    recovery of 1-3 KiB fits either; a traceback of more than ~4 KiB after a second refusal may not.
    Stock gives such a program nothing past the cap.
  - THE WINDOW's overrun residual stays: probe2k's 785 string / 163 record landings are the kernel's
    2 KiB slice taking the heap past the window between resumes, now at a lower reserve top (one
    record run that ended inside at baseline ends down under id for that reason, off 23824: a paint-
    opened second chance, then the dispatcher's pack refused 100 B past the new ceiling).  F1's
    burst-top landing stays.
  - S1 is counted, not treated.  "No reserve for a doubling" was considered and dropped: the shipped
    reserve is what lets the next new string's retry of a doubling <= G/2 succeed; without it the
    refusal is sticky until a sweep or a drop.  If the machine's strtab count is nonzero, the fix is
    in lj_str_resize (a failed growth keeps the old table; the retry per new string is then one
    refused allocator call and the shim's recogniser keeps it off the program's account) -- a LuaJIT
    patch in the anchored style, deferred until the count says it is needed.
  - The kernel thread: no rule.  Its refusals are pcall(main) -> the machine down whatever the tier
    (K1/J3/J4), and its only non-fatal absorber is the recorder, which rule 1 covers on any thread.
    A kernel-thread rule would also break mem_test's unarmed cases (W8 and most others run on the main
    thread with wd_depth 0, where the predicate reads "kernel").
  - The in-machine extra refusals (5/4/3 against pf = 1) are not explained by this design; nothing
    here depends on them.  The instrument's recorder, kernel and strtab flags are what will.
  - Not measured: near-wall time (expected no worse: fewer cycles), the Linux build (compiled nowhere
    here; the diff touches no platform code), the full negative control (five new sabotages run by
    hand; the 35 existing ones re-anchored by inspection only).
  - The instrumented copy's OCLJREF credit/top/over fields re-compute the OLD arithmetic; under rule
    3 they overstate the top by G - od_credit.  The OCLJID line carries od_credit; the absorber
    classification (stream order) is unaffected.
