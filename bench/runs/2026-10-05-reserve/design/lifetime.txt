(a) THE MECHANISM TARGETED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every paint- and recorder-absorbed refusal in 28 672 hermetic runs was a VERDICT refusal
(forensics REPORT F3: 2 303 + 25, win0 = 2), and lj52_gc_refused executes it whoever caught
it: gc_odstate = RESERVE, the window shut, a cycle armed (map 1.0).  The reserve tier then
raises the sandbox's top from total + G/2 to total + G -- G/2 more, 16 KB at the 192 KB
stick and 256 KB at 8 MB -- and nothing closes it until a proof finds the heap under the cap
(THE CREDIT, lj52shim.c:960-964), which a fill that keeps its data never lets happen.  So a
fill whose refusal the probe's pcall(paint) absorbed (2 169 of 2 192 second chances, F3) or
the JIT recorder dropped (23) grows G/2 further and arrives at the RESERVE top, where the
verdict is played again, and there it lands outside the handler in the OpenOS-shaped string
cell 21 of 228 times (F4, probe2oe:86 and :81), and in the machine 3 of 21 (results-wall-
window, "THE WINDOW's downs": a paint-absorbed refusal, the reserve tier, a last proof
200-2 068 B past its top, a 56 B refusal outside any handler).  The reserve exists for
"catch, format, drop, carry on" (map section 7), which needs tens of bytes (W8) to a few KB
(a message printed through the tty); the allocator cannot tell the program's catcher from
the paint's (map L8: not recommended), so the lever here is the SIZE the reserve has
whoever opened it: the second chance should buy little.  THE WINDOW already gives every
top the same 4 KiB of lent room before its cycle decides; the reserve is made that same
quantity, placed past the heap the refusal found so that a refusal at the window's ceiling
gets it in full.  Its LIFETIME (close it at a resume: map L4) was also prototyped and is
reported in (d.4): it does not move the stalls, and the mechanics say why.

(b) THE RULE CHANGE, AS A DIFF AGAINST native/lj52shim.c (3186e87), AND THE BOUND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rule.  When a refusal opens the reserve tier (BURST -> RESERVE), the tier's credit past
the cap is set ONCE to
      gc_rsv = max(used - total, G/2) + LJ52_GC_RSV        (LJ52_GC_RSV = 4 KiB, one window)
and read, wherever the reserve top is used, as
      lj52_gc_rsvcredit(M, G) = clamp(gc_rsv, G/2, G).
A later refusal in the tier shuts the window and arms, as before, and does not re-size it.
The fresh-record rule (W11) sets gc_rsv = G: that tier was opened by history, not a refusal.
The clamp's floor keeps a refusal from ever shrinking the room (a big request refused from
under S); its ceiling is the old absolute bound, so a cap changed since the tier opened
re-clamps it (G is passed in, never cached).  Nothing allocates; two scalar writes.  Zero
LuaJIT lines.  No new hook: the kernel's slice, THE WINDOW, THE CADENCE and the flush are
unchanged in code -- they read the top through the same two sites they always had.

streams (sc/forensics/out/oe12032, and run 4096 of L4_string_j1) show the verdict's refusal

the reserve closes the top returns to total + G/2 while the fill's live data is already
past it, so the verdict forms at the first proof after the resume and its refusal lands
wherever the fill is -- which, right after a resume, is the dispatcher's pack, the step's
closure, the checkArg closures or the record: the outside sites.  R0 shows the contrast:
with the top left AT the refused heap (not under the live data) the fill must add live data
before the verdict can form, and it adds it in the batch -- 0 of 803.  L4 is rejected by
evidence, like L6; combining it with the size (R4K+L4) was therefore not built: the closing
still puts the top under the live data.  A per-byte-spent reserve was not built either: it
is the same top with no credit for garbage collected, strictly less friendly to a
recovery, and nothing above suggests it would change the landing.

d.5  The size, R.  R0 (no room past the refusal) is the destructive control: it fails W21
(the recovery that holds across cycles is refused inside its report) and W20's non-vacuity
clause.  R1K fails W21 and W16Rj/W16RjL.  R8K passes everything but W20/W20L and the re-
scoped W7, which pin 4 096 by design (as W19 pins 11 KB), and buys +138-174 string objects;
it is the conservative alternative if the judges want a print's worth of room twice over.
R4K is proposed: one window, the quantity THE WINDOW already deems "the room a crossing
gets before its cycle decides", a quarter of the old floor, 1/64 of the old maximum.

Builds: V (THE WINDOW after the review, the shipped shim; additive ae3e414b / dropin
7fa4e8f9) and L (V + this diff, additive and dropin), both ALSO as the instrumented native
(OCLJ_REFLOG=1, the forensics' src with mklt.py applied: src-instr-R4K/) so every refusal's

C03C03, the 192 KB stick at ramScaleFor64Bit 1.8 (G at its floor), batch 10, junk 24
(OCLJ_CAP_BATCH/OCLJ_CAP_JUNK), the watchdog kernel.  Cells: JIT on x {record, string}, 15
reps each per build; JIT off x string, 10 reps (the cell where V took the second-chance path
8 of 10, all clean); the dropin JIT on record, 5 reps.  Interleaved by rep, V and L
alternating.  Plus the standard matrix (batch 100: 192/256/1024 KB, four shapes, JIT on and
off, the dropin at 192 and 1024) once per build for R12, and the full suite.
Pass criterion (ALL must hold; each can fail; the control that shows it can is named):
  (1) THE BOUND.  Every L run's od_peak <= G/2 + 4096 + 2 * 4096 = 28 672 B, unless its
      OCLJREF seq=1 line shows kernel=1 or used > cap + G/2 + 4096 (the kernel's slice
      spent past the sandbox's ceiling), in which case <= G + 4096.  CAN FAIL: V's second-
      chance runs read od_peak >= G + 1 648 (the three downs) and G/2 + ~16 K in the clean
      ones; the same check on V must fail in >= 10 of its 30 JIT-on runs or the chain is
      void.
  (2) WHAT THE SECOND CHANCE BUYS.  In each cell, the median held count of L's runs whose
      first refusal was absorbed outside the fill (OCLJREF seq=1 with at= not in the fill's
      chunk lines, or refusals = pf + 2 with pf >= 1 before the done string) exceeds the
      median of its single-refusal runs by at most 150 objects (string) / 40 (record); V's
      the same statistic must exceed 200 / 40, else the probe took no second chance there
      and the cell is void for (2).  CAN FAIL: V.
  (3) BAD OUTCOMES.  Over L's 30 JIT-on amplified runs plus the dropin's 5: STALL + DOWN <=
      V's count in the same chain, and 0 PROC-DEATH, and 0 RECOVERY-REFUSED anywhere (the
      probe's own catch-drop-format must keep working: that is W8/W21 in the machine).
      The design's own claim, judged only if V shows >= 2 bad in its second-chance runs: L
      shows <= 1 bad among its second-chance runs.  If V shows < 2, add 15 reps per JIT-on
      record cell to both before judging (3); if still < 2, (3) is reported, not judged.

(g) COSTS AND RESIDUALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The second chance is bounded, not removed.  A fill that never saw its refusal still
    holds 4 KiB + a window more (70-110 string / 10-13 record objects at 192 KB) and still
    meets a verdict at the reserve top; hermetically that verdict landed outside the
    handler 6 times in 803 second chances (shipped: 46), and the machine is untested.  The
    explanation offered for the reduction (d.3) is a hypothesis about the probe's phase;
    the gate's (3) is where it is judged.
  - A program that catches its refusal and holds its data while allocating more than
    4 KiB + a window of LIVE data across the cycles that costs is refused inside its
    recovery, where the shipped shim allowed G/2 (16-256 KB) and stock allows nothing.
    W21 pins the floor at ~2 KB kept; a print through the tty is mostly garbage, which the
    armed cycles collect under the top.  If the judges want more, R8K passes everything but
    the two cases that pin 4 096, and costs +70 string objects of creep.
  - With the JIT on, 1 KiB is too small (W16Rj): the recorder's own allocations share the
    reserve.  4 KiB passed W16Rj/W16RjL in 96 caps x 2; the margin above 1 KiB is not
    measured finer than that.
  - The kernel-garbage residual (THE WINDOW's: the slice overrunning the window at the
    dispatcher's pack, 725 of 2 048 caps in that probe) is untouched; its second-chance
    share falls 103 -> 60 only because the fill spends fewer resumes in the reserve tier.
  - In the kernel-overrun case the new bound is the absolute one, through the clamp; the
    tighter bound holds when the sandbox's own refusal opened the tier.  Both are stated
    in THE CREDIT and tested (W7 re-scoped, W7k, W20).
  - Tests: W19 and W7 re-scope (both demonstrated passing on R4K and, as re-scoped, W19 on
    the shipped shim); W7k's precondition should read the tier; W16R's "covered" topr
    should too (no landing reaches it).  `unbounded`'s expected set needs re-verifying.
  - Cost in code: one constant, one field, one 4-line helper, four one-line call sites, one
    5-line block in lj52_gc_refused, a 14th _OCLJ_WALLSTATS value; zero LuaJIT lines; no
    allocation; no back-off; no knob; no new hook; collections -1 %; identical behaviour for
    every program that sees its own refusal.
  - Not addressed: the recorder-opened second chances (23 of 2 192) are bounded like the
    others but the recorder still opens the tier (map L1 is the lever for that); the
    extra in-machine refusals (5/4/3 against pf = 1) are not explained here.
