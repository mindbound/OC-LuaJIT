-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) THE MECHANISM TARGETED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE WINDOW decides at a proof that the live data does not fit under the tier's top S
(gc_win = 2, "the verdict"), and the shim executes that decision at THE NEXT CROSSING OF S,
whoever makes it.  In lj52shim.c :1239 the lend refuses any crossing while gc_win == 2 &&
gc_low > top; lj52_gc_refused (:1251-1263) then counts, opens RESERVE and shuts the window
"whoever ends up catching the error" (map REPORT 1.0).  The forensics show this is the whole
second-chance path: every paint- and recorder-absorbed refusal in 28 672 hermetic runs was a
verdict refusal, win0 = 2 (F3: 2 303 + 25 of 2 328); the opener of the second chance was the
probe's pcall(paint) in 2 169 of 2 192 and the JIT recorder in 23 (F3, F5); the deterministic
case F1 is the same decision executed by event.timer's 64 B record, allocator call 28749,
seven calls after the window opened at the recorder's GCtrace (call 28742) -- the verdict is
reached within a handful of allocations of the crossing that opened the window, so where it
lands is a lottery over the next few allocation sites: the batch (good), the paint (absorbed
-> reserve tier for a fill that never saw it), event.timer or the dispatcher (outside).
The OpenOS-shaped probe's 21 stalls in 228 second chances are all the RESERVE-top verdict
landing at the timer's record after a paint-absorbed BURST-top verdict (F4, analysis-extra).
The map's section 7 fixes what must survive: the reserve tier exists for the program that
CAUGHT its own refusal ("catch, format, drop": W8 with a proof between format and drop;
W2b/W2c's arm; W11's fresh record).  Its section 1.1.4 gives an exact, no-patch predicate
for the recorder (lj_vm_cpcall's frame: nres < 0 and saved PC == L, with J->state not idle
and vmstate not C/GC/EXIT), proven against ground truth on 31 000 of 31 000 refusals (F7).
So the design changes only the DELIVERY of the verdict: a verdict is a finding about the
program's data, and it is served to the handler that holds that data.  Three conclusions
from the evidence shaped it:
  - "a Lua frame, not a fast function, not under a C frame" (one of the task's suggestions)
    cannot address it: 921 of the 2 303 paint refusals were at probe2.lua:46/:44, plain
    concatenations in a Lua frame, and event.timer's record is a Lua-frame TNEW;
  - "after the next resume boundary" cannot either: the first allocations after a resume are
    the dispatcher's (table.pack: the k2048 down site, F4);
  - the only thing that separates pcall(paint) from pcall(fill) is WHICH PROTECTED CALL the
    request runs under; both are pcall'd Lua functions on one coroutine.  The map's L8 says
    this is a policy choice about who the program is.  It is; the policy here is the one a
    byte count would make: the program is the handler under which most of the growth past
    the cap happened.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(b) THE RULE, AND THE DIFF
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Notation as d2-final: T the cap, G = clamp(T/16, 32K, 512K), S the tier's top (T + G/2
BURST, T + G RESERVE), L = LJ52_GC_LEND = 4 KiB, K = LJ52_GC_KSLICE = 16 KiB.  New constant
H = LJ52_GC_HOLD = 8 KiB, with a compile-time guard L + H <= G_min/2 (12 K <= 16 K).

R1  THE VOTE.  On the slow path (a growth that would pass T; both paths, after the look)
    the shim reads the identity of the request: (the running thread, the function the
    innermost pcall/xpcall frame called -- its GCproto for a Lua function, so a fresh closure
    per step is one handler; NULL when nothing in the coroutine protects it).  It is read
    from the frame links the VM maintains (the frame carrying FRAME_PCALL is the callee's:
    vm_x64.dasc ff_pcall, lj_err.c err_unwind), from jit_base on a trace as lj_err_mem does,
    64 links at most, with the instrument's own guards (tvisfunc on every slot, the base
    inside the stack); an unreadable frame means "addressed" (today's behaviour).  One
    candidate and one count (Boyer-Moore majority); a proof that finds the heap under the
    cap resets the count.  The request is ADDRESSED when it comes from under the handler of
    record and is not the trace recorder's own (the forensics' cpcall predicate).  The
    kernel's requests are addressed as before.
R2  THE HOLD.  A verdict refuses only an addressed crossing.  Any other crossing under a
    standing verdict is HELD: lent on, gc_win left at 2, within the window and, in the
    BURST tier, LJ52_GC_HOLD past it; in the RESERVE tier within the window only (that
    window is the sandbox's bound).  A hold that meets that ceiling is refused and counted
    (holdfails); that refusal opens the reserve tier as any does.  A STANDING VERDICT STANDS
    through the proofs the held lends arm, until a proof finds the heap under the top: THE
    WINDOW's re-decision `used - grown > top ? 2 : 1`, read again at such a proof, demotes
    it to "open", because the held lends it has since collected and whatever the kernel
    holds live between resumes both sit in `grown`; a demoted verdict lets the next
    non-addressee crossing meet the plain 4 KiB ceiling instead of the hold's room (the
    dispatcher's pack after the kernel's garbage: measured as revision 2's sandbox downs,
    section (d)).  Two consecutive cycles proved the data; a third past the top does not
    unprove it.  (Revision 3 of the prototype; the one-line amendment is in the diff.)
R3  THE RECORDER'S REFUSAL OPENS NOTHING.  A refusal of a request the recorder makes (on
    either thread) counts and arms as every refusal does (W2b/W2c), but does not open the
    reserve tier and does not shut the window: trace_abort drops the error, nobody recovers
    from it, and the program sees the next one at the same top, as stock would.  Every other
    refusal, the hold's ceiling included, opens the reserve as today.
R4  THE KERNEL'S SLICE PAST THE HOLD.  The kernel's BURST credit gains H (T + G/2 + K + H),
    so it keeps K - L = 12 KiB past whatever the sandbox was lent, in both tiers; THE
    CADENCE's regime-P kernel top moves with it.  Its RESERVE credit is unchanged.

THE BOUND (unchanged, R5):
    sandbox:       used + delta <= T + G + L          BURST hold ceiling T + G/2 + L + H <= T + G + L  <=> H <= G/2  (guarded)
    every thread:  used + delta <= T + G + K          kernel BURST top T + G/2 + K + H <= T + G + K    <=> H <= G/2
    the kernel keeps K - L = 12 KiB past the sandbox's ceiling in both tiers (W19, and the
    #error at compile time, unchanged).  The verdict can still only refuse; a hold is a lend
    under a ceiling; caught refusals cannot ratchet anything (W7, W7k pass unchanged).
    Measured: W7 od_peak G + 4 078 as before; W22 (below) the kernel's 11 KiB table granted
    with the sandbox 2 KB past its window; mem_test's W18 kernel refused 0/0/0/0.

What is NOT in the design, and why: "an absorbed refusal opens nothing" for Lua-level
absorbers (the task's fourth suggestion) was revision 1.  It cannot tell a paint from the
program's second handler, and it cost the latter its recovery: W24's sandbox died (a program
whose growth moved from one of its own pcalls to another was refused at the ceiling with no
reserve, then refused again on 'err: ' .. msg).  Revision 2 keeps "opens nothing" for the
recorder only, where it is exact, and gives the hold its room instead.

(f) THE IN-MACHINE GATE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Layout: d2-final section 8's amplified capacity probe as it was run for THE WINDOW's gate
(192 KB stick, scale 1.8, OCLJ_CAP_BATCH=10, OCLJ_CAP_JUNK=24, string and record, arms O/D/L,
10 reps O and D, 5 L; interleaved by rep), with the shipped THE WINDOW build (V) as the
CONTROL in place of stage C, and THE ADDRESS build (A) beside it; plus the batch-100 standard
matrix for A (68 runs, chain T's layout) for capacity, last-five and idle.  The harness reads
_OCLJ_WALLSTATS to 19 values and CAP-GC gains holds=, recref=, votes=, addressed=, holdfail=.
A "second chance" in a run = CAP-GC refusals >= 2 with OCLJCAPX pf >= 1 or trace aborts
between the fill's refusals (the control's definition from the results doc); under A the
same event reads holds >= 1 && refusals == 1 ("the paint's verdict was held").
Pre-registered pass criterion (all must hold):
 (a) the gate can fail: the control shows >= 8 second-chance runs in its 15 amplified JIT-on
     record runs (V read 9, W 12).  Below 6 the amplification did not transfer: VOID.
 (b) A: second-chance runs in the same cell at one-sided Fisher p < 0.01 against the
     control's (8 -> at most 1 of 15; 10 -> at most 2); and across all 50 amplified A runs
     0 DOWN, 0 PROC-DEATH, at most 1 STALL, with no bad run showing holdfail > 0 (a down with
     holdfail > 0 is the hold's own hazard, revision 1's: the gate FAILS on one).
 (c) attribution: in every A run with holds >= 1, refusals == pf-independent 1 and the fill
     ended inside ("done/..." painted, class CLEAN); addressed == 1 at the end of every
     clean run (the fill was the handler of record when it was refused).
 (d) capacity (batch 100): A >= stock in every cell, and A's single-refusal held count
     within 1 % of the control's like for like; in the amplified JIT-on record cell A's
     MEDIAN held count >= the control's median of its single-refusal runs - 1 % (A will read
     below the control's mean: the second-chance runs' extra G/2 is gone by design).
 (e) last five batches: A's ratio to same-run stock <= 1.05x the control's in every cell
     (the frame walk's cost); idle arms per window within +-10 % of the control's, 0
     refusals at idle; the full suite 66/0 (additive), the dropin and JIT-off suites as the
     control's; mem-2 and km-1 as before.
 (f) the bound: every A run od_peak <= od_limit + kslice; the kernel never refused
     (CAP-GC's kernel figures).
What the main session's instrumented in-machine run can settle first, and should: the
allocation COUNT per handler per step in the real probe (the paint's five component calls
against the fill's ten records).  The vote is a majority of past-cap growths; my count
estimate is fill ~100, paint ~45, step ~10, dispatcher ~5 per step, a majority but not a
landslide.  If the paint out-allocates the fill in the machine, A delivers as today there
and (b) will not be met; that is the design's stated limit (g), not a defect to be hidden by
the gate.  The OCLJREF addr=/rec= fields and OCLJHOLD lines of W/src-vref give that answer
run by run.

(g) COSTS AND RESIDUALS, HONESTLY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Costs:
 - 96 lines of shim code and a 60-line comment; a new concept ("the handler of record")
   that is a HEURISTIC about who the program is -- the majority of growths past the cap by
   protected call.  It is right for the capacity probe (hermetically: 100 % of 10 cells) and
   for any program whose data grows under the handler that also makes most of its
   allocations; it is wrong for a quiet producer behind a chatty consumer (then the verdict
   is delivered as today, by sequence -- no regression, no gain) and it is late by up to
   L + H = 12 KiB for a program whose growth moves to a second handler of its own (W24:
   refused at the hold's ceiling with its recovery intact).
 - A frame walk inside the allocator on every growth past the cap: 1-4 links as a rule,
   bounded at 64, reading what lj_err_mem and err_unwind read; the same reads the forensics
   instrument made on 31 000 refusals.  It is a new category of code in the allocator
   (frame reads), and a reviewer should treat it as such.  Measured cost: not separable
   from noise hermetically; the in-machine last-five ratio is the test.
 - The kernel's burst-tier top gains 8 KiB (C6b re-scoped); the every-thread bound and the
   kernel's 12 KiB guarantee are unchanged.
 - Six more _OCLJ_WALLSTATS values (19, under LUA_MINSTACK), the harness reads them.
 - Capacity in runs that had a second chance drops by the G/2 the absorbed refusal bought
   (hermetically 53-295 objects; in the machine's amplified JIT-on record cell this was 12
   of 15 runs on W, 9 on V): the amplified cell's mean will read lower than the control's.
   That is stock's parity, which the row asks for; it must be said so the gate is not
   misread.
Residuals:
 - A hold that overruns its room (burst: L + H = 12 KiB; reserve: the window, 4 KiB) is
   refused at the ceiling in whoever's code that is and opens the reserve tier as today: the
   second chance returns there.  Hermetically never; in the machine the between-step code
   is estimated at ~4 KB (paint ~3, event.timer 0.8, dispatcher 0.3), inside the burst
   room and at the reserve window's edge.  The reserve-tier hold only matters after a
   program's own caught refusal that kept its data (W16R's shape).
 - Nothing here reaches a refusal no window was open for: W9's class, and the kernel's
   garbage between resumes past the window (the k2048 downs that are not second chances:
   725 of the string cell's 776 and all 162 of the record cell's are the baseline's own).
 - The design took three revisions to get the hold right (the room, the kernel's slice
   past it, the standing verdict), each forced by a hermetic measurement that the first
   six cells could not have shown; the in-machine between-step footprint is the one
   number this chain still rests on an estimate for, and the gate's holdfail count is
   what will say whether 12 KiB of room was enough.
 - A verdict that rests on garbage pinned across two cycles is still a verdict; it now
   reaches the program's handler instead of a bystander, which is better, not right.
 - The recorder on the kernel thread: its refusals open nothing now (R3), but a kernel-thread
   recorder crossing is still refused where it happens (no window for the kernel), and the
   penalty/blacklist cost of that abort (map L1b) is untouched.
 - The identity is a pointer compared, never followed; a prototype freed and reused costs at
   worst one verdict served as today.
 - Not run: the Linux build (compile only, not attempted here), the Minecraft harness.
