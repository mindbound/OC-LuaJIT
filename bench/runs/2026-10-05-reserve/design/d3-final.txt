1. THE DECISION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 What is built

THE RESERVE'S SIZE: when a refusal opens the reserve tier, the tier's credit past the cap is set
ONCE to max(used - total, G/2) + LJ52_GC_RSV, and read everywhere through a clamp to [G/2, G].
LJ52_GC_RSV = 8 KiB (two windows; section 1.3).  A fresh record's reserve (W11) stays G.  Nothing
else about the tiers, THE WINDOW, THE CADENCE, the kernel's slice or the flush changes.  Plus
one graft from THE RECIPIENT: HOOK_VMEVENT is handled exactly as HOOK_GC (no credit, no window,
no reserve).  Zero LuaJIT lines; about 30 code lines; two scalar writes on the refusal path, one
4-line read helper at four sites, three one-line guards; nothing allocates; no knob; no back-off.

1.2 Why this, and why not the others

The in-machine forensics (NOTE.txt) fixed what the designers did not have: in the machine every
absorbed refusal is a 56-64 B closure the kernel's machine.lua makes on the sandbox thread inside
a component call (wrapUserdata :1261, unwrapUserdata :1280, checkArg :117), caught by the probe's
pcall(paint); the recorder absorbed 0 of 50, the string table 0 of 50; at the reserve top the fill
got 2nd-4th chances as long as the verdict kept landing on those closures.  Against that record:

  - THE RESERVE'S SIZE (lifetime R4K = identify rule 3) is the only lever that reaches the
    in-machine absorber and is unconditional: whoever caught the refusal, what the second chance
    buys falls from G/2 + a window (16 KiB at the floor, 256 KiB at 8 MB) to R + a window, and
    the sandbox's bound after a refusal tightens from total + G + 4 KiB to max(u, S) + R + 4 KiB.
    Measured (two independent implementations, identical aggregates to the digit): every cap
    whose program saw its own refusal is byte-identical (9 399 of 9 399 batch-caught caps over
    seven cells; j3-evidence E1 corrects lifetime's "10 240"); 0 of 10 240 runs past either
    bound; collections -1 %; capacity -0.06..-0.68 % per cell, all of it in second-chance runs.
    Every new check has been seen failing in the direction it guards (R0 fails W21, R1K fails
    W16Rj/W16RjL/W21, five sabotages each caught by a named case).  The read-side clamp
    (lj52_gc_rsvcredit) is taken over identify's store-side one: j3-safety M-I1 (a lowered cap
    or a future RESERVE write without the credit breaks the bound; R4K's helper prevents both).
    R4K's floor form max(u - total, G/2) + R is taken over identify's clamp(u + R - total, G/2,
    G): a refusal below the burst top keeps S + R, so a 16 KiB doubling caught at cap + 2 KiB is
    still granted on retry at the floor (j3-practice 4.3).

  - THE ADDRESS (the handler-of-record vote) is REJECTED as the base and DEFERRED as a design.
    Its hermetic record is the best of the round (second chances 0 in all eight cells, every
    refusal caught by the batch, 16 173 quiet caps identical), and it is the only lever that
    removes the lottery instead of bounding its prize.  It is not built because: the vote has
    no decay, so after one episode on a near-cap machine a new filler meets the hold's ceiling
    before it can become addressee (M-A1); the identity is execution-mode dependent -- a
    trace-inlined pcall is invisible to the walk from jit_base, and OpenOS's dispatcher is that
    shape (M-A2); a frame walk in the allocator on every past-cap growth rests on invariants
    LuaJIT does not itself exercise on this platform and was tested on probe shapes only
    (M-A3); when the vote is wrong the design is today's defect plus 8 KiB, and in the machine's
    own amplified cell the paint (~50-70 allocations per step through the invoke wrapper)
    plausibly out-allocates the fill (~80) -- the designer's own void condition (M-A4, E11);
    the rawverdict negative control fails 0 of 87, reproduced, so THE WINDOW's two-cycle rule
    becomes untestable until an unbuilt W26 exists (M-A5, E14); the reserve-tier hold is one
    window against OpenOS's handler-copy loop, and revision 1 measured +122 downs there (j3-
    practice 1.5 B); "441 -> 0" is zero by construction (E8); near-wall timing n = 2 (E13).
    What is taken from it: the instruction to settle the per-handler allocation count with the
    instrument (section 6), and the gate clause style "a bad run with the design's own hazard
    flag set is reported as that hazard, never absorbed into a pass".

  - THE RECIPIENT's rule 1 (the recorder's refusal opens nothing): exact and proven both ways
    (ctl_rec2 7/7; dbg_r1b2 and 1 092 program-visible RECORD-state refusals read 0; the literal
    sabotage stalls W16Rj).  DEFERRED: 0 of 50 in-machine refusals were the recorder's; its
    hermetic reach is 23 of 2 192 second chances; it costs lj_frame.h, a 12-line predicate on
    the refusal path and the W16R family's tier read (j3-practice G-defer, j3-evidence E22).
    With the reserve sized, a recorder-opened second chance costs R + a window like any other.
    Trigger to land it as its own change: a nonzero recorder= count in any in-machine chain's
    OCLJREF stream.  Rule 2 (HOOK_VMEVENT) is TAKEN: three lines mirroring HOOK_GC, free, and it
    keeps the harness's own jit.attach trace counter from opening the reserve during the very
    measurement the gate makes.  The S1 recogniser is NOT taken: the NOTE settled its question
    (no in-machine refusal's request equals a table doubling; the extra refusals are placed).

  - L4 (close the reserve at the next resume): built by lifetime, 39 of 803 stalls (the shipped
    shim's 46; p = 0.25), +26 objects bought; the mechanics say why (closing puts the top under
    the live data, so the verdict forms at the first proof after the resume -- the dispatcher's
    pack, the step closure, event.timer).  REJECTED by evidence.
  - L6 (close at the next proof regardless): closereserve fails W8 W11 W16Rj W19 W7k.  REJECTED.
  - R0 and R1K: W21 fails (the holding recovery is refused inside its report); R1K also stalls
    W16Rj/W16RjL with the JIT on (the recorder's allocations share the reserve).  REJECTED.
  - Identifying Lua-level absorbers by chunkname (map L8): a policy about who the program is,
    fragile; not prototyped by anyone.  NOT TAKEN.

1.3 The constant: 8 KiB, not 4

Lifetime pinned 4 KiB (one window) and showed its floor: W21 (~2 KB held, ~8 KB granted across
the proofs the report costs) passes at 4 KiB, fails at 1 KiB and 0; W16Rj fails at 1 KiB with the
JIT on.  Both other judges asked for more: j3-safety M-L1 (the 4 KiB floor is at the edge of a
real holding recovery, unmeasured; "pin 8 192 if not measured") and j3-evidence E3 (R8K's only
failures are the design's own 4 096 pins; it passes W21, W16Rj and the bound).  The decision
rests on three things:
  (a) What R buys is bounded creep, not fewer lottery draws.  The draws at the reserve top are
      THE WINDOW's: after an absorbed verdict refusal the window shuts and reopens up to top +
      LJ52_GC_LEND, whatever the top; R moves the top, not the 4 KiB above it.  R0, R8K and R1K
      all read 0 of 803 hermetic second-chance stalls over three seeds; R4K read 6, all one
      six-cap pocket at one seed (off 9600-9680, j3-practice confirmed one pinned); the shipped
      shim 21 / 0 / 25.  That is phase arithmetic of the probe, not a property of R; it does not
      transfer to a machine whose absorber is a 3 KB paint through the invoke wrapper.  So the
      hermetic stall count does not choose R; the recovery's room and the creep do.
  (b) The error is asymmetric.  Too small, R refuses a program's own catch handler inside its
      report -- the contract failure THE CREDIT exists to prevent (R1b; 20 of 60 runs ended that
      way before stage B).  Too large by 4 KiB, a fill that never saw its refusal holds +70
      string / +13 record objects more at the floor, in second-chance runs only, invisible to a
      player who never hit the wall.  By code reading a catch handler that prints
      debug.traceback(err) through the tty holds the traceback (~1 KB) plus g->tmpbuf (~1 KB,
      halved only at atomic) plus a file handle if it logs: ~2-2.5 KB, against 4 KiB a 1.7x
      margin and against 8 KiB 3.5x.  Nobody has measured it; section 5 (7) does.
  (c) The price is stated.  The tightened sandbox bound at the floor becomes total + G/2 +
      16 KiB = total + G, 4 KiB under the old ceiling instead of 8 KiB under; at 8 MB it is
      total + 272 KiB against 516 KiB.  W20's pin and the re-scoped W7's bound move to 8 192.
      Hermetically R8K's W20 reads +8 KiB bought (lifetime (e.2)); its full eight-cell census is
      owed (section 6) -- identity on non-second-chance caps holds by construction (the rule
      touches nothing before a reserve opens) and the bound is arithmetic, so what the census
      must show is the creep and the k2048 cells.
LJ52_GC_RSV = 2 * LJ52_GC_LEND, written as a literal (8 * 1024) with the relation in the comment
and two compile-time guards: never under one window (the measured JIT floor), never so large
that R + LEND exceeds G_min/2 (the tightened bound must be real at the smallest G, not the
clamp's).  Lowering it to one window later is a one-constant row that section 5 (7)'s
measurement can open; it is not done here.

1.5 What this buys in the machine, stated before the gate runs

  - A fill whose first refusal the paint absorbed holds R + a window past the heap it was
    refused at (12 KiB at any cap) instead of up to G/2 + a window past the cap (20 KiB at the
    floor, 260 KiB at 8 MB).  In the amplified JIT-on record cell (12 of 15 runs took the path
    on W, 9 of 15 on V, 12 of 15 in chainR) the cell's mean capacity falls by the difference;
    that is stock's parity and the row's purpose.
  - The sandbox's excursion after a refusal is bounded at max(u, S) + 12 KiB, measured per run
    from the instrument, instead of total + G + 4 KiB.
  - The reserve top's lottery -- the verdict landing on the kernel's closures until the ceiling,
    then wherever the ceiling is crossed -- is NOT changed in expectation.  History: 3 downs in
    46 second-chance JIT-on record runs across W, V and chainR.  No 30-60-run chain can tell
    3/21 from 1.5/21; section 5 reports the count and judges only a regression bar.  THE
    ADDRESS is the lever for that lottery; its two inputs are measured in this chain so the
    decision to build it is made on data.

2. THE RULES, IN THE SHIM'S VOICE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 THE CREDIT, the RESERVE bullet (replaces lj52shim.c:960-963 in place)

 *   - RESERVE, after a refusal: LJ52_GC_RSV past the heap the refusal
 *     found -- never under the burst tier's top, never past total + G.
 *     The refusal opens it; a proof that finds the heap back under the
 *     cap closes it.  A proof that does not -- the cycle ran before the
 *     program dropped its data -- leaves it open, which is what lets
 *     "catch, format the message, drop, carry on" work.  THE RESERVE'S
 *     SIZE (2026-10-05): it was G/2 more past the cap, whoever caught the
 *     refusal.  In the machine the catcher is usually not the program:
 *     the first refusal of a fill lands on a 56-64 B closure machine.lua
 *     makes inside a component call on the sandbox's thread
 *     (wrapUserdata, unwrapUserdata, checkArg), under whatever pcall
 *     wraps that call -- the capacity probe's pcall(paint), OpenOS's
 *     dispatcher -- and the fill never hears.  It then held 16-256 KB
 *     more than its own refusal allowed and met the reserve top's
 *     verdict wherever it fell (3 of 30 amplified JIT-on record runs
 *     down; bench/results-wall-window-2026-10-05.md).  The allocator
 *     cannot tell that catcher from the program's, so the reserve is
 *     sized for what a recovery needs, not for a tier: tens of bytes
 *     (W8) to a few KB held while a message is formatted and printed
 *     (W21: 2 KB held across the cycles its report costs).  Two windows
 *     is that with room to spare, a half of the old floor and 1/32 of
 *     its ceiling.  Sized ONCE, at the refusal that opens the tier:
 *     later refusals in it shut the window and arm, as before, and
 *     cannot creep it (W20, W7);

2.2 THE CREDIT, the guard sentence (lj52shim.c:982-983)

 * No credit at all where nothing could repay it: under HOOK_GC (a finalizer;
 * PUC's cap is hard there too), under HOOK_VMEVENT (a VM event handler --
 * jit.attach's, the harness's trace counter; its error is dropped with "VM
 * handler failed" and would open the reserve for a program that never saw
 * it) and under a host GCSTOP.

2.3 THE CREDIT, the bound sentence (lj52shim.c:983-988)

 * The bound is absolute, not incremental: used + delta <= total + G + the
 * slice for every growth outside the norefuse window -- the sandbox's own,
 * total + G + LJ52_GC_LEND, inside it, and after a refusal at heap u,
 * max(u, total + G/2) + LJ52_GC_RSV + LJ52_GC_LEND, i.e. total + G/2 +
 * LJ52_GC_RSV + 2 * LJ52_GC_LEND when the sandbox's own refusal opened the
 * tier (W20, W7) -- so caught refusals cannot ratchet it (mem_test W7,
 * W7k), and the excursion is charged -- getFreeMemory reads 0, both Java
 * sides clamp it there.

2.4 The constants (after the KSLICE #error block, lj52shim.c:1128)

#define LJ52_GC_RSV    (8 * 1024)       /* THE RESERVE'S SIZE: two windows (2 * LJ52_GC_LEND) past a refusal */
#if LJ52_GC_RSV < LJ52_GC_LEND
#error "the reserve a refusal opens must be at least one window: at 1 KiB the recorder's own allocations stalled W16Rj"
#endif
#if LJ52_GC_RSV + LJ52_GC_LEND > LJ52_GC_ODMIN / 2
#error "the reserve a refusal opens must fit inside the old tier at the smallest G (W20, W7), or the tightened bound is only the clamp's"
#endif

Both must be seen to fire (set 1 * 1024, then 16 * 1024, compile, restore) before the first
mem_test run: every gate here was born broken in one direction.

2.5 The helper's and the sizing block's comments

/* THE RESERVE'S SIZE: the reserve tier's credit -- what the refusal that
 * opened it set, never under the burst tier's, never over G.  G is passed
 * in so a cap changed since then re-clamps it, and no path can read a
 * credit the bound does not cover whatever gc_rsv holds.  See THE CREDIT. */

  /* THE RESERVE'S SIZE: once, past this heap.  `used` is the heap the
   * refused request found; a refusal under the burst top (one big request)
   * keeps that top plus the room, so its retry is not refused for being
   * early.  Clamped to G where it is read. */

2.6 The bound, per thread class (T the cap, G = clamp(T/16, 32 KiB, 512 KiB), S = T + G/2,
    L = LJ52_GC_LEND = 4 KiB, R = LJ52_GC_RSV = 8 KiB, K = LJ52_GC_KSLICE = 16 KiB; u the heap
    at the refusal that opened the reserve tier; every growth outside the norefuse window)

  sandbox, burst tier:                  used + delta <= S + L                        (unchanged)
  sandbox, reserve opened by a refusal: used + delta <= max(u, S) + R + L
                                                     <= S + R + 2L = T + G/2 + 16 KiB  (u <= S + L
                                                        for a sandbox refusal)
                                        and always   <= T + G + L                    (the clamp)
  sandbox, reserve by the fresh-record rule:          <= T + G + L                    (unchanged)
  the kernel (wd_depth 0, or the outermost arm's thread), either tier:
                                        used + delta <= T + tier + K, tier in {G/2, clamp(rsv)}
                                                     <= T + G + K                    (unchanged)
  the kernel's margin past the sandbox's ceiling:     K - L = 12 KiB in both tiers  (unchanged,
                                                        #error-guarded, W19)
  under HOOK_GC, HOOK_VMEVENT or a host GCSTOP:       used + delta <= T  (no credit, no window)
  Exception, named in the gate: when the heap was already past S + L when the tier opened (the
  kernel's slice spent past the sandbox's ceiling between resumes, or the kernel's own refusal
  at S + K), u is what the kernel left and the clamp at G binds: the absolute bound is the one
  that holds there (observed max credit 24 704 = G/2 + 8 320 at R = 4 KiB; at R = 8 KiB the
  kernel's own refusal sizes G/2 + 24 KiB, clamped to G up to G = 48 KiB).
  Everything is charged: getFreeMemory reads 0.
