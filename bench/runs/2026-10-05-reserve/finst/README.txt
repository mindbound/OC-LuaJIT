Build into an OC native:
  OCLJ_SHIM=<this dir>/src  sh native/build-native.sh      (with OCLJ_VARIANT=additive or dropin)
  Enable with OCLJ_REFLOG in the JVM's environment before the state is created (1 refusals; 2 + proofs;
  3 + arms); each state prints "OCLJREFINIT| st=... level=..." once -- the fingerprint that THIS native is
  the one loaded.  A diagnostic copy: never ship.
Generated by tools/mkref.py from the repo's lj52shim.c with tools/frag_{types,fields,protos,hooks}.c, which
tools/frag-from-forensics.py derives from the forensics' fragments (sc/forensics/tools) by anchored splices.
ANCHORS: every one of the forensics' eight anchors matched the final source exactly once, unchanged; one
splice was ADDED (9: oclj_rsv_note at the end of the sizing block in lj52_gc_refused, after gc_rsv is set,
before the tier is opened).  mkref.py refuses a source without lj52_gc_rsvcredit and the sizing line.
What differs from the forensics' instrument (REPORT.txt section 1 describes the rest, unchanged):
  - oclj_credit (the read-only re-computation of lj52_gc_credit that gives OCLJREF's credit=/top=/over=)
    follows the final shim: no credit under HOOK_VMEVENT; in the reserve tier the credit is
    lj52_gc_rsvcredit's (clamp(gc_rsv, G/2, G)); a fresh record past the burst top (which the real credit
    moves to the reserve with gc_rsv = G) gives G, as before;
  - OCLJREF gains  rsv0=<gc_rsv before the refusal>  rsv=<gc_rsv after it>  tier=<the sandbox's credit in
    its tier after it: lj52_gc_rsvcredit(G) in the reserve, G/2 in the burst -- _OCLJ_WALLSTATS' 14th
    value's arithmetic with the refusal's total>.  So on the refusal that sizes the reserve rsv0 != rsv and
    tier = clamp(rsv); on later refusals in the tier rsv0 == rsv (W20: it cannot creep);
  - the OCLJXP line at the sizing block (OCLJ_REFLOG on, like every other line):
      OCLJXP| fired=1 kind=rsv rsv=<gc_rsv> used=<heap found> total=<cap> over=<used - total> top=<total +
      clamp(rsv)> G=<G> calls=<allocator calls>
    (lifetime's line, with over=; compare_F.py reads rsv/used/total/top as compare_lt.py did).
  The record struct (oclj_refrec) gained the three fields; the ring API (oclj_refring/oclj_gcevring) is
  unchanged.  The environment variable is read the gate-safe way (GetEnvironmentVariableA / a scan of
  environ): build-native.sh's getenv gate passes.
Structural check (logs/diff-repo-vs-instr.txt): diff repo -> src is PURE ADDITIONS, no line of the repo's
