                the kernel's slice applied (lj52_gc_kernel); wd_depth; the running thread is wd_by[0];
                is the main thread; the pointers
  vm vmstate    g->vmstate decoded (INTERP C GC EXIT RECORD OPT ASM, or TRACE with vmstate = trace no.)
  jst jstate    J->state decoded (IDLE ACTIVE RECORD RECORD_1ST START END ASM ERR)
  incp cfnres recorder
                the innermost C frame is a cpcall frame (saved PC == L, nres < 0); recorder=1: the
                refusal was raised INSIDE THE TRACE RECORDER'S PROTECTED CALL (incp, J->state not idle,
                vmstate not C/GC/EXIT) -- trace_abort drops that error; the program never sees it.

recorder and seq fields separate all of these in the machine.

Verdict on the lever, hermetically: restricted to the recorder it is surgical and harmless here, but
it reaches 23 of 2 192 second chances (1 %).  The other 2 169 were opened by the paint absorbing a
verdict refusal, and every remaining second-chance outside landing (19 oe stalls, 103 k2048 downs)
is one of those.  In the machine all three second-chance downs showed pf = 1 (a paint absorption);
if the in-machine OCLJREF lines show the paint as the opener there too, this lever does not reach
them.  What would: making a refusal that some non-program handler absorbs not count as the
program's (it is the VERDICT's refusal in every absorbed case here), which this experiment did not
test.

8. WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH

- Nothing here ran in the machine; the in-machine second-chance downs (3 in 30) are not reproduced

- The heartbeat, OpenOS-timer and kernel-garbage probes are models of the machine, not the machine.
- One seed and one capk (384); the census's other seeds/capk were not swept.
- The Linux build of the instrumented source was compiled, not run.
- For the in-machine run, per down: take the OCLJREF lines of the machine's st=; opened=1 marks
  the refusal that opened the reserve tier; its recorder= (1: the recorder dropped it) and at=
  (paint's line in the probe chunk, an OpenOS file, or =machine with kernel=1/kthr=1) name the
  absorber's site; win0=2 says it was a verdict, over > 4096 with win0 != 2 a window overrun;
  the lines after it, up to the down, are the "extra" refusals.  Run with OCLJ_REFLOG=1 (2 adds the
  proofs, enough to see each verdict being formed).
