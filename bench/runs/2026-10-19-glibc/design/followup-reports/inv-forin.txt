SUMMARY: The diag/4-identity flake comes from the test, not the serializer, and there are two independent causes. Neither is Linux-specific: the pristine forin.lua failed 20 of 2000 fresh diag processes on AlmaLinux 8, 18 of 2000 on Ubuntu and 26 of 2000 on Windows [M] (bench dirs */census-orig). (1) JIT: hot counters are shared by PC hash, so about 1 process in 100 starts a trace in the identity shape. It stitches across coroutine.yield, and its exit leaves a CONT frame for lj_cont_stitch under the yield. That coroutine then suspends 3 slots higher, and its blob is 20 bytes longer (536 vs 516) [M]. (2) Localisation heuristic: when the two in-process PCs differ in only 1-2 bytes and the PC's own low byte is 0x04, two windows qualify ("2 candidates") [M]. The writer is faithful. Stitched and unstitched blobs restore in fresh processes on all three platforms and resume to the same 3,4,5,6,DONE [M]. The recommended test-only fix has two parts: jit.off(chunk, true) for the identity shape, and a GC64-address filter on candidate windows (top 17 bits clear). With it: 0 of 2000 failures on each platform; 20 of 20 passes under forced hotloop=1, where the pristine/unfixed check fails 59 of 60; and every real identity break still fails 20 of 20 per arm.

PRODUCT_ISSUE: NONE. The flake is the test instrument: JIT nondeterminism in the identity shape plus an ambiguous byte-localisation heuristic. It occurs at the same rate on Windows, Ubuntu and AlmaLinux 8 [M]. The serializer encodes both VM states faithfully, and both restore to identical behaviour [M]. One documentation inaccuracy, not a behaviour difference: serializer/README.md's known limitation says a thread blob carries one process-specific word per Lua frame. A continuation frame also puts its raw words on the wire through the slot loop: the lj_cont_* machine address (measured 0x40d2a8 = lj_cont_stitch in Alma's non-PIE test binary; in the shipped PIC .so it would be an ASLR'd code address) and the contpc heap pointer. A stitch frame's trace slot goes out as nil, because lua_type reports LJ_TTRACE as nil [S]. Like the link, these are inert: u_thread pass 3 overwrites f-4..f [S][M].

-- open_questions
  * Which hot counter triggers in the unforced case is not pinned down [I]. The forced run shows the loop's ITERL at diag_wrap:11 recording, and a function-entry trace at diag_wrap:4 is also possible. The fix covers both, because jit.off(chunk, true) sets PROTO_NOJIT on every nested prototype.
  * Should the inert raw frame words be zeroed on the wire (the README's 'write nil there instead' item)? It would make thread blobs deterministic across processes and remove the lj_cont_* code address, which reveals the .so's load address. It is a wire-format change, and outside this test fix.
  * The other diag cases (1-wrap-warn, 3-wrap-refuse, and so on) still run with the JIT on. They passed in every failing process measured, so they are left JIT-on deliberately. Should a later change apply nojit to all diag shapes for determinism?
